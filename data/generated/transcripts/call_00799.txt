I am cautious with attribution because some customers would have returned anyway, but the timing is encouraging. The lapsed-customer offer had a lower response and a higher discount cost.

Luis Romero (Customer Success Manager): That nuance is useful. What did the team learn from the weaker campaign?

Sophie Bell (Marketing Manager): The segment was too broad. Someone included customers whose last purchase was only forty-five days ago, and they were not really lapsed. They got a discount they did not need. We changed the rule to ninety days and excluded recent email engagers.

Luis Romero (Customer Success Manager): Would a reusable approval checklist help?

Sophie Bell (Marketing Manager): Yes, especially for promotions. We need a standard check for stock availability, exclusions, consent, and margin. At the moment each campaign manager asks a different person.
