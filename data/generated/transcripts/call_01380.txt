Amelia Hart (Account Executive): What feels hardest today?

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): When did that become visible?

Roy Collier (VP Marketing): During the product launch last quarter. The central team built the nurture, Australia changed the qualification threshold, and Germany used a separate landing page. Reporting showed three different conversion rates for what was supposed to be one program.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): That would make expansion difficult to justify. Are there parts of the current system that the teams value?

Roy Collier (VP Marketing): Yes. The lead alerts are faster than our old process, and the sales teams like seeing activity before a call. The problem is that only some regions have clean data.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): Is the data issue source quality, integration timing, or process discipline?

Roy Collier (VP Marketing): All three, probably. Our ERP sync is stable, but CRM ownership fields are inconsistent. Some distributors are treated as customers in one region and prospects in another.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): That sounds like a business-definition issue before it becomes a platform issue.

Roy Collier (VP Marketing): Exactly. I don’t want to buy more seats to automate confusion.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): Fair. What would need to be true for renewal to be approved?

Roy Collier (VP Marketing): First, a credible governance plan. Second, evidence that the sales teams will use the handoff data. Third, a financial case that does not depend on a perfect database.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): Could we work through those one at a time? For governance, would a central template library and approval workflow address the issue?

Roy Collier (VP Marketing): It could, but regional leaders will resist if central approval adds days to every campaign.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): Then perhaps approval is only required for shared assets and regulated claims, while local campaigns use guardrails and naming standards.

Roy Collier (VP Marketing): That’s closer. We tried a global committee last year and it became a queue. Nothing launched quickly.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): Understood. For adoption, how do you measure whether sales uses the handoff data?

Roy Collier (VP Marketing): We can see alerts delivered, but not whether a rep acted on them. The sales operations team has a meeting-created field, though it is not populated consistently.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): We could define a small set of follow-up outcomes and make them visible in the weekly review. Would sales operations support that?

Roy Collier (VP Marketing): They might. Their director is skeptical because previous dashboards created more arguments than action.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): Then the first report should focus on exceptions: accepted leads without an owner, high-intent activity without follow-up, and records with conflicting statuses.

Roy Collier (VP Marketing): That is more useful than another funnel chart. What about expansion into the distributor channel?

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): I would not recommend expanding there until the identity and consent model is agreed. Distributor contacts can have different permissions and ownership rules.

Roy Collier (VP Marketing): Good. The distributor team is pushing hard, but they have a separate CRM instance in two countries.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): That integration boundary is a material risk. We can scope a discovery phase, but I would not include it in the renewal value case as if it were ready.

Roy Collier (VP Marketing): Finance will appreciate that honesty. They are comparing renewal against making no decision for another year.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): Could we quantify hours spent on cleanup and delayed follow-up, without claiming all of that disappears?

Roy Collier (VP Marketing): I want the model to show the downside too. If adoption remains uneven, expansion should pause.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): Perhaps Australia and the United Kingdom, because the teams already use the platform regularly. Germany could be a later test once the localized approval requirements are documented.
