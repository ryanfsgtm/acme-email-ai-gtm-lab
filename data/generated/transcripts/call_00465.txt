Alexandre Volkman (Demand Generation): Shared templates, common naming, one view of lead status, and consistent reporting. The country teams still need different offers and languages. We do not want a central team approving every subject line.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): So governance at the framework level, autonomy at the campaign level.

Alexandre Volkman (Demand Generation): Exactly. We currently have several tools and a lot of spreadsheets. The monthly report is assembled by hand, and the numbers disagree depending on which export someone starts with.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Which outcome would justify a purchase?

Alexandre Volkman (Demand Generation): Reliable funnel reporting first. Faster campaign launches second. I know vendors usually lead with journey orchestration, but my director will ask where sourced pipeline came from and whether the number is comparable month to month.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): We can build the proposal around reporting and then show the workflow benefits. What budget range has been discussed?

Alexandre Volkman (Demand Generation): Nothing approved. The director has a planning envelope, but finance will want evidence that this replaces enough manual work and improves visibility. Procurement also prefers a predictable annual amount.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Would a phased subscription help? For example, a core reporting and campaign workspace for the regional team, followed by additional country users after adoption.

Alexandre Volkman (Demand Generation): Maybe. The risk is that a small first phase gives us another partial system. If the data model and reporting foundation are complete from day one, a phased user rollout could work.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That’s an important distinction: phase the users, not the foundation. How many systems would we need to connect initially?

Alexandre Volkman (Demand Generation): CRM, website forms, webinar registration, and the existing email platform. There is also a retail partner feed, but that can wait. We need to know whether the first four produce trustworthy source and consent fields.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Who would approve the data model?

Alexandre Volkman (Demand Generation): Marketing operations would propose it, demand generation would test it, and privacy would review consent. Sales operations needs to agree on lifecycle stages. If any one of those groups is left out, reporting arguments begin within a month.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): What is the procurement process once the business case is accepted?

Alexandre Volkman (Demand Generation): First, an internal request and budget owner. Then finance checks the commercial case, procurement compares suppliers, legal reviews terms, and IT checks integration. We are not a huge company, but the sequence is not instant.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Are there preferred contract terms?

Alexandre Volkman (Demand Generation): Annual billing, a clear renewal notice period, and no ambiguous overage charges. We also need implementation services itemized. My director dislikes proposals where the subscription looks affordable and the real cost appears later.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): I can separate platform, onboarding, and optional services. Would you expect a competitive evaluation?

Alexandre Volkman (Demand Generation): Yes, though not necessarily a formal tender. We will compare the incumbent email vendor, one larger suite, and a specialist platform. The specialist may be easier for operators; the suite may be easier for finance to rationalize.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): What would operators care about during that comparison?

Alexandre Volkman (Demand Generation): Building and testing journeys, reusable segments, previews, and the speed of correcting an error. They will not be persuaded by a beautiful executive dashboard if a campaign takes three days to validate.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): And what would executives care about?

Alexandre Volkman (Demand Generation): Attribution, reporting consistency, and whether the investment supports the regional growth plan. They want a concise answer to “what did marketing contribute?” Operators want to know whether tomorrow’s launch is safe and manageable.
