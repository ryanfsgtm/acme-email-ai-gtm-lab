Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Good morning, Anahi. How are things at Treutel, Mann and Green? I saw the spring term is already underway.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): That sounds like progress. Since this is a renewal and expansion conversation, I’d like to understand what is working, what still causes friction, and which additional teams might benefit.

Anahi Lebsack (Implementation Partner): The core programs are stable. We use the system for prospective-student nurture, event reminders, and follow-up after counselor conversations. The biggest improvement is that our coordinators can see the next action instead of asking implementation to interpret every contact history.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): How has adoption been across the education programs?

Anahi Lebsack (Implementation Partner): Uneven at first. The admissions team adopted it quickly because the journeys matched their calendar. Continuing education was slower; their events are less predictable, and they wanted more flexibility. We held two working sessions and created a simpler template for them.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): What did the simpler template change?

Anahi Lebsack (Implementation Partner): Fewer branches, clearer ownership, and a pause option around school holidays. The original version was technically impressive but too busy for people who only build a campaign once a month.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): That is useful feedback. Are coordinators creating those campaigns themselves now?

Anahi Lebsack (Implementation Partner): Most of them. Implementation still reviews consent, sender details, and the handoff rule. We do not want every person improvising those parts.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): How are you measuring the impact?

Anahi Lebsack (Implementation Partner): We track inquiry-to-appointment and appointment-to-enrollment. The numbers are not perfect because some counselors update the CRM late, but the trend is better. We also spend less time answering “what happened to this person?”

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Would better reporting be part of the expansion case?

Anahi Lebsack (Implementation Partner): Yes, but not as a giant dashboard project. Our director wants a monthly view by program and source. The practitioners want faster workflow editing and easier testing. If we add reporting without fixing the last little operational annoyances, adoption will flatten.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Which operational annoyance is most important?

Anahi Lebsack (Implementation Partner): Previewing a message with the right personalization and seeing exactly which branch a test contact takes. Sometimes a coordinator says, “I thought this person would receive the reminder,” and we have to retrace three conditions.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): We can review the test panel and branch explanations. Are there any new use cases under consideration?

Anahi Lebsack (Implementation Partner): Alumni engagement and donor communications. Those audiences have different consent language and ownership. We are not ready to combine them with admissions, but we want to understand whether separate workspaces make sense.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Separate workspaces could isolate permissions and templates while allowing central governance. We would need to confirm the data model and the reporting roll-up before recommending a structure.

Anahi Lebsack (Implementation Partner): Good. I do not want a sales answer that says everything can be merged. Sometimes separation is the safer design.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Agreed. What would a sensible pilot for alumni look like?

Anahi Lebsack (Implementation Partner): A small event series. Invite alumni, track registrations and attendance, send one follow-up, and create a task for the relationship manager when someone requests a conversation. No broad donor segmentation in the first phase.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): That gives us a bounded test. We can also document the suppression rules and the ownership transition.

Anahi Lebsack (Implementation Partner): Please include a fallback if the relationship manager is not assigned. We have a few programs where ownership is still manual.
