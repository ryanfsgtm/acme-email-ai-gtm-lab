Lori Blick (Security): Marketing asked for better nurture campaigns and less manual list handling. The immediate trigger was a product webinar where an unsubscribed contact received a reminder. It was embarrassing, though not a breach.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): What did the team do afterward?

Lori Blick (Security): They checked the list manually and added another spreadsheet column. That solved the next send, but obviously it is not a system. I’m interested in automation, but I’m also wary of automating a bad process.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): What would a good process need to guarantee?

Lori Blick (Security): Consent status must be authoritative, unsubscribe events must flow back, and someone must be able to explain why a person entered a campaign. We also need a clean offboarding process.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): Which system currently owns consent?

Lori Blick (Security): That is one of the unresolved questions. The website stores form consent, the CRM stores a checkbox, and our email tool has suppression records. They do not always agree.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): That sounds like the first design decision. Would you prefer the CRM to become the source of truth?

Lori Blick (Security): Maybe. Marketing will say yes because they live in the CRM, but I do not want convenience to decide policy. We need to compare timestamps, wording, and purpose. A generic checkbox should not automatically authorize every campaign.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): Understood. We can document consent by purpose and source. What data would you consider in scope for phase one?

Lori Blick (Security): Name, business email, company, country, and lifecycle stage. Possibly product plan. Not support notes, not free-text call summaries, and not raw usage events until we understand the retention impact.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): Would country-level segmentation be sufficient for the first campaigns?

Lori Blick (Security): Probably. Marketing wants individual behavior triggers, but I think that is a phase-two conversation. We have enough work just reconciling contacts.
