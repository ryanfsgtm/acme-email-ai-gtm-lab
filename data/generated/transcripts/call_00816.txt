Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): How are campaigns requested and approved?

Fabian Blanda (Demand Generation): Regional marketing submits a brief. Legal checks language and consent. Brand checks design. Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): And reporting?

Fabian Blanda (Demand Generation): We report influenced pipeline, sourced pipeline, MQL volume, conversion, and speed to follow-up. The problem is not a lack of charts. Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): That sounds like a governance problem, not simply a dashboard problem.
