Priya Shah (Account Executive): Antonette, thanks for fitting this in. I saw that Dooley and Sons is evaluating automation while the Marketo renewal is still under review. What prompted the evaluation now?

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): The renewal date is part of it, but not the whole story. We have grown the sales team, and the handoff from marketing to sales is becoming inconsistent. Some leads get a fast response; others sit in a shared queue until someone notices them.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Is the inconsistency mainly volume, or are the routing rules different by product line?

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): Both, probably. We manufacture components for several industries, and the forms capture end use, company size, and urgency differently depending on which page someone visits. Marketo has rules, but they have accumulated over time. I am not sure anyone can explain all of them.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): When a lead sits untouched, how do you discover that?

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): Usually through a sales manager asking why an account has no activity. We do not have a reliable response-time report. There is a dashboard, but it shows created leads and MQLs, not whether a rep actually accepted the assignment.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): So the operational question is less “how many leads did we generate?” and more “did the right person take ownership quickly?”

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): Exactly. And we need that by region. Our UK team works differently from the export group, and a single global threshold creates arguments.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): What does your current lead lifecycle look like?

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): Inquiry, marketing qualified, accepted, opportunity, closed. In practice, “accepted” is sometimes a stage and sometimes an email reply from a rep. We have reporting that treats those as interchangeable, which is not ideal.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Would you want the platform to enforce acceptance, or simply measure it first?

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): Measure first. If we enforce a rule before agreeing what acceptance means, people will work around it. I want two weeks of clean data and then a decision.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That is a sensible sequence. What else is driving the review besides routing?

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): Attribution. Our finance director wants to know which programs create pipeline, but the source values are messy. “Trade show,” “trade-show,” and the event name are separate values. We can report them, but it takes a manual cleanup every month.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Have you tried standardizing those values at the form level?

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): We tried. Then a distributor sent a spreadsheet with their own labels, and someone imported it directly. The cleanup problem came back. We need controlled values and a way to identify exceptions, not just a style guide.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): We can discuss validation rules, import governance, and an exception queue. Who would own those decisions after launch?

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): Revenue Operations, which is me and one analyst. We can own the model, but we cannot become a permanent help desk for every sales rep who forgets a field.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Then user experience matters. How much flexibility do reps need to edit a lead?

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): They need to add notes and update disposition. They should not edit source, region, or lifecycle definitions. Those fields affect reporting and routing.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Understood. Marketo may already support parts of that. Are there specific frustrations with it today?

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): The interface is not the biggest issue. The bigger issue is that changes depend on one consultant who knows the old setup. When she is unavailable, simple updates wait. We want something our team can maintain without reverse-engineering it.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): How quickly do you need internal changes made?

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): Campaign copy can wait a day. Routing changes tied to a new sales territory cannot. We had a territory split last quarter and ran the old rules for four days.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That sounds expensive. Did leads have to be reassigned manually?

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): Yes, and that created duplicate outreach in a few cases. Nobody lost a major deal, but it made the company look disorganized.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Besides Revenue Operations, who needs to evaluate a replacement or expansion?

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): Marketing, sales operations, IT, and procurement. The sales director cares about response time. Marketing cares about campaign flexibility. IT will ask about identity, data export, and integrations. Procurement will compare the total cost with renewing Marketo.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Are you expecting a full replacement, or could this be a focused change around routing and reporting?

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): We are open. A full replacement is attractive if migration is manageable, but I do not want to spend six months recreating every historical workflow. A phased approach might be easier to approve.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): What would a phased first release include?

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): Standardized intake, regional routing, acceptance tracking, and a basic attribution model. We could leave advanced nurture programs for later. The first release has to improve handoffs without disrupting active campaigns.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): How many active campaigns are there now?

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): Around twenty, though only six matter weekly. The rest are long-tail programs that someone may revive. We also have a backlog of old contacts, and their consent history is uneven.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Would you migrate all historical contacts immediately?

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): Probably not. We need a policy decision. Some older contacts are useful for account history, but that does not mean they should enter a new nurture. I would rather stage them and require a review.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That distinction should be in the implementation plan. What would make you comfortable recommending a next step?

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): A working session with our analyst where we map three real lead paths, including one messy distributor import. I also want a transparent migration estimate: records, workflows, fields, exclusions, and who does what.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): I can arrange that. We will bring a solutions consultant and use your examples. We should also define success metrics before the session—perhaps assignment accuracy, time to acceptance, and reduction in manual reassignment.

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): Add attribution completeness and the percentage of exceptions resolved within a day. If those are not measured, they will drift.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Good additions. One question: if the system improves reporting but requires more manual entry from reps, would that be acceptable?

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): Only temporarily. We can tolerate a short adoption period, but a new system that depends on perfect rep behavior will fail. It should capture what it can automatically and make the required steps obvious.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That's helpful. We will focus the demonstration on normal workflows and edge cases, not just a polished campaign.

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): Please include the territory split example. I want to see what happens when ownership changes while a lead is already in progress.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): We will. I'll send a recap with the evaluation group and proposed agenda. Are you aiming for a decision before the Marketo renewal date?

Antonette Streich (Revenue Operations): Ideally, but the decision may slip if procurement needs another comparison. Our priority is a sound process, not an artificial deadline.
