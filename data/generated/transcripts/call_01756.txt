Luis Romero (Customer Success Manager): That sounds like a possible place to simplify. What does sales say is missing?

Raymundo Fisher (VP Marketing): They say marketing sends names, not context. I think that is partly fair. Luis Romero (Customer Success Manager): Understood. How many people would actually use a marketing automation platform day to day?

Luis Romero (Customer Success Manager): Would cleaning that data be part of the project or a separate exercise?
