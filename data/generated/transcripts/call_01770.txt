Jarrell Wilkinson (Sales Operations): A visible explanation. The rep should see which actions contributed, the relevant campaign, and whether the contact fits the target account. A black-box number is not enough.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): What were the practical failure points?

Jarrell Wilkinson (Sales Operations): Too many approval steps, unclear ownership, and a testing process that required specialists. A campaign could be technically correct and still arrive two weeks late. Workflow speed matters because our news cycle moves quickly.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): How do operators test today?

Jarrell Wilkinson (Sales Operations): They send internal test emails and inspect a few records. Branching logic is hard to validate. One team has a spreadsheet of test cases, but it is not maintained. I hesitate to promise that a new platform will solve this automatically.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): That caution is warranted. We should test with representative journeys and agreed acceptance cases rather than rely on a feature checklist. What journey would be a good pilot?

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Excellent. What would success look like?

Jarrell Wilkinson (Sales Operations): Operators build the journey without engineering help, compliance can inspect the consent logic, sales receives an explainable handoff, and marketing can report on influenced pipeline without manually reconciling three exports.
