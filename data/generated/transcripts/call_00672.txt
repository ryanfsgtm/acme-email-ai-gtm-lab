Priya Shah (Account Executive): Did we provide enough training?

Terence Purdy (Implementation Partner): We provided training. I am not sure we provided the right training. It was organized around all the available functions, not around the three tasks the store teams cared about. There is a difference.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That is fair. What did the client say about results?

Terence Purdy (Implementation Partner): They liked the open rates and said the reporting was clearer than before. But revenue attribution was incomplete, and the finance lead did not accept the numbers as proof.
