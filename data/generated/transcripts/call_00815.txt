Leland Krajcik (Privacy): Yes. Start with existing customers and clearly consented event contacts. Defer older prospect records until we can establish provenance. That is less exciting, but it gives us a controlled first release.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Which countries are in the first phase?

Leland Krajcik (Privacy): Marketing operations owns the CRM extract. Customer support owns customer status. Event marketing owns attendance and consent records. Privacy reviews the purposes and exceptions; we should not become the operational owner by accident.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That separation makes sense. What controls do you expect in the platform?

Leland Krajcik (Privacy): Field-level access where possible, suppression of withdrawn consent, audit logs for changes, and a clear way to honor deletion requests. We also need users to understand that an unsubscribe is not merely a marketing preference if another system has a broader restriction.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Should approval be required for every campaign?

Leland Krajcik (Privacy): For the first phase, yes. Later we can define a low-risk category with delegated approval. I would rather review the process while the audience is small.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): What reporting would privacy need?

Leland Krajcik (Privacy): Monthly exceptions: contacts suppressed, failed deletion actions, records with unclear consent, and users who changed sensitive fields. I do not need a dashboard full of vanity engagement metrics.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): We can create an exceptions report and a separate marketing report. For retention, do you have a standard period?

Leland Krajcik (Privacy): It varies by purpose and record type. Event leads may be retained for a shorter period than active customer contacts. The exact periods are being confirmed with counsel, so the implementation should support rules rather than hard-code one number.

Leland Krajcik (Privacy): Website forms, yes, but only after the notice language is updated. Event platform, probably a nightly import first. Real-time synchronization would be nice, but it is not worth making the launch dependent on a connector we have not tested.
