Rylee Hills (Security): It varies. The web team has one preference center, one acquired brand has another, and some regional teams still export lists for local sends. Amelia Hart (Account Executive): That sounds like a governance problem as much as a campaign problem.

Rylee Hills (Security): Yes. And if a platform makes it easy to launch before those definitions are settled, people will launch. That is my fear.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): We can discuss permissions, approval steps, audit logs, and data handling. Before I go there, which systems would need to connect?

Rylee Hills (Security): CRM, the commerce platform, the preference center, and probably the service system. There are also regional data stores. I do not want a sales presentation that says “just send everything into the new platform.”

Rylee Hills (Security): Part of it. I would need to know whether those timestamps are immutable, who can alter a consent state, and whether an administrator can export a full customer file without a second person involved.
