Giuseppe Corkery (Implementation Partner): That is still being calculated. We have around 300 employees, but the customer file is much larger. Loyalty members, online buyers, anonymous visitors—different consent states.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Consent segmentation will affect the design more than the employee count. Do you have a current estimate for marketable profiles?

Giuseppe Corkery (Implementation Partner): Roughly 1.4 million, but I wouldn’t put that in a contract yet. The number includes dormant profiles and records with incomplete permissions.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Understood. We can create a pricing band and identify the fields that must be validated before final quotation.

Giuseppe Corkery (Implementation Partner): Procurement will ask for a fixed three-year total. They don’t like usage surprises, especially with a retail calendar that spikes in November.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): We can propose a committed base with a seasonal overage cap. Another option is a higher annual tier with no event-based adjustment. I’ll show both so the trade-off is visible.

Giuseppe Corkery (Implementation Partner): The overage cap sounds more honest. The higher tier will look efficient until someone asks why we paid for January capacity in July.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That’s a fair point. Are there any implementation services procurement expects inside the subscription price?

Giuseppe Corkery (Implementation Partner): They will expect the vendor to contribute. The business case won’t pass if services appear as a second large invoice.

Giuseppe Corkery (Implementation Partner): Austria, because the local team has an old agency workflow and insists on reviewing every audience manually.

Giuseppe Corkery (Implementation Partner): Exactly. Braze has been telling the team that their implementation partners can move quickly. Everyone loves that sentence until the first data issue appears.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Our proposal will include assumptions and dependencies. If the customer file is not normalized, the date moves; that should not be hidden in a footnote.

Giuseppe Corkery (Implementation Partner): Good. Security will also ask where data is stored and how deletion requests are handled. I don’t own those answers.

Giuseppe Corkery (Implementation Partner): Another concern: local teams need help during the first major promotion. If support is only business-hours in one time zone, that won’t work.
