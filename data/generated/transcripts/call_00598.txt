Priya Shah (Account Executive): Maximillian, thanks for making the time. I know renewal discussions can feel like another item in an already crowded planning cycle. How are you doing?

Maximillian Reilly (VP Marketing): Well enough. We’ve just finished the student recruitment campaign review, so I may sound more opinionated than usual.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Opinionated is useful. I’d like to review what Bailey - Ullrich has achieved with the platform, hear where the team is frustrated, and explore whether expansion makes sense. If it doesn’t, we can say that plainly.

Maximillian Reilly (VP Marketing): I appreciate that. The honest answer is that the platform is now part of our operating rhythm. Campaigns get out more consistently, and we have fewer last-minute requests to IT. But there are still gaps that make me hesitant to broaden usage.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): What gaps are most visible to you?

Maximillian Reilly (VP Marketing): Reporting and governance. We can show activity, but I’m not always confident we can show a clean connection from engagement to enrollment. Also, individual schools create their own variations. That makes the overall picture noisy.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Is the noise mostly naming, audience definitions, or different goals?

Maximillian Reilly (VP Marketing): All three, though the goal issue is the biggest. A postgraduate program may define success as an application, while continuing education cares about attendance at an information session. The dashboard treats them as neighbors when leadership treats them as different businesses.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That suggests the expansion conversation should start with a measurement framework, not more seats. How many schools are actively using the system?

Maximillian Reilly (VP Marketing): Four regularly, two occasionally. The central team supports all of them. The occasional users tend to return to their older tools when deadlines get close because they know those tools, even if the process is less controlled.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): What would make the central team comfortable supporting them?

Maximillian Reilly (VP Marketing): Templates that are genuinely reusable, permissions that are easy to understand, and training that happens close to the moment of use. We did a broad training session last year, and people remembered the concept but not the clicks.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): We can redesign enablement around role-based clinics. One for campaign builders, one for reviewers, and one for reporting owners. Would that fit your calendar?

Maximillian Reilly (VP Marketing): Probably after the next intake cycle. Right now we are protecting the team’s capacity. The phrase “just one more workshop” makes everyone nervous.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Fair. Let’s not make the renewal dependent on a workshop schedule. On the product side, which functions are underused?

Maximillian Reilly (VP Marketing): The lead scoring configuration is mostly untouched. We tried a model, but the admissions team objected that it made applicants look like sales prospects. That language caused a lot of resistance.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): We could rename the model and align it to recruitment stages, but the concern sounds conceptual as well as linguistic.

Maximillian Reilly (VP Marketing): It is conceptual. An applicant is not a lead in the same way a software buyer is. We need to be careful not to import a sales funnel where it doesn’t belong.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That makes sense. Perhaps the better use is prioritization of follow-up, with transparent criteria and no automatic judgment about a person’s value.

Maximillian Reilly (VP Marketing): That framing would be easier. We also have privacy review requirements. Any expansion has to fit our consent and retention policies, and those vary slightly by program.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): I’ll bring in our privacy specialist before we define scope. What expansion would actually help this year?

Maximillian Reilly (VP Marketing): More central reporting, possibly the events team, and better integration with the student information system. I’m less interested in adding every department immediately.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Why the events team?

Maximillian Reilly (VP Marketing): They are still managing registrations in a separate tool. After an event, the follow-up is a manual export. We lose context, and sometimes two people contact the same attendee.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): If we connected event attendance to the existing communication history, would that remove a meaningful amount of work?

Maximillian Reilly (VP Marketing): Yes, if the integration is reliable. Reliability is the key phrase. I would rather have a daily verified sync than a supposedly real-time connection that silently fails.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): I agree. We’ll assess the sync with a failure alert and reconciliation report. No point in increasing volume if nobody knows whether the data arrived.

Maximillian Reilly (VP Marketing): Exactly. The other issue is cost. We’re not opposed to investing, but the expansion needs to be staged. Our finance director will reject a broad commitment based on hypothetical adoption.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): We can propose a base renewal with a defined event-team pilot and an option to add the student information system work after technical validation. Would a pilot need a separate approval?

Maximillian Reilly (VP Marketing): It would need budget visibility, but not necessarily a separate procurement process if the commercial terms are clear. Please show the ceiling before we spend time on design.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): I’ll prepare two scenarios: renewal only, and renewal plus the staged pilot. I’ll include assumptions, implementation effort, and what would trigger the next expansion decision.

Maximillian Reilly (VP Marketing): Good. I don’t want a discount conversation to obscure the operating questions.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Understood. Is there anything that could put the renewal itself at risk?

Maximillian Reilly (VP Marketing): Not the relationship. The risk is that the platform becomes another system we pay for but only central marketing can use. If the schools don’t see practical value, the board will ask why we expanded.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Then adoption evidence belongs in the renewal pack. We can show active programs, time saved where measurable, and the remaining barriers without pretending every team is mature.

Maximillian Reilly (VP Marketing): That’s the right balance. Also, please include a timeline. Our renewal date is not the same as the academic planning date, and people keep mixing those up.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): I’ll separate commercial dates from the intake calendar. I’ll send the draft next week, then we can review it with finance and the central operations lead.

Maximillian Reilly (VP Marketing): Add the privacy officer too. If they join late, we lose another month.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Good call. I’ll include them from the start. Thanks for being direct today.

Maximillian Reilly (VP Marketing): It’s easier when the questions are specific. We’re open to continuing, but the expansion has to earn its place.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That’s a sensible standard. I’ll follow up with the two commercial paths and the technical questions.
