Amelia Hart (Account Executive): Understood. What happens today when someone asks not to receive marketing messages?

Jamar Heidenreich-Kreiger (Privacy): Ideally, the request goes to marketing operations, they update the CRM, and the change is reflected in the mailing list. In practice, there can be a lag.
