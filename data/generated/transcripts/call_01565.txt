Amelia Hart (Account Executive): Sensible. The current scope includes the campaign builder, templates, reporting, and standard onboarding. Would you need additional seats immediately?

Caroline Cole (VP Marketing): We have four core users and three reviewers. The reviewers need visibility, not full editing access. Pricing seven full users would make the internal comparison look worse than it should.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): I’ll separate full users from reviewers in the quote. How will the reviewers judge success?

Caroline Cole (VP Marketing): They’ll want reliable campaign and pipeline reporting. Our CFO will ask whether we can connect spend and outcomes without a spreadsheet assembled by hand.

Caroline Cole (VP Marketing): Better than last year, not perfect. We have regional naming differences. “Partner,” “channel,” and “distributor” are sometimes used for the same source.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): Then the first report should include a data-quality note and a mapping exercise. I don’t want a polished chart that implies more certainty than the source data supports.

Caroline Cole (VP Marketing): Agreed. Finance is skeptical of dashboards that never show a caveat.
