Priya Shah (Account Executive): Lyla, good to see you again. How is the holiday planning going?

Lyla Roob (Revenue Operations): Busy. Retail never really pauses, even when everyone says the quarter is over. We are preparing the January promotions now.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): I reviewed the last quarter's numbers. Overall send volume is up, automation usage is healthy, and the regional teams are building more journeys without central help. How does that look from your side?

Lyla Roob (Revenue Operations): Adoption is good, yes. The teams are pleased with the workflow. But I want to talk about delivery before we celebrate too much.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Absolutely. What are you seeing?

Lyla Roob (Revenue Operations): The UK and Benelux accounts with the largest audiences have seen inbox placement fall. At first we thought it was campaign content, because the creative was heavier than usual. Then we compared the smaller accounts and saw their placement was mostly stable.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Do you have the domain-level breakdown?

Lyla Roob (Revenue Operations): I do. Gmail is the clearest signal, but Outlook is not great either. The biggest senders are landing in promotions or spam more often, and complaint rates are climbing. It is not every customer, which makes it harder to prioritize.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Have you changed sending cadence or list sources?

Lyla Roob (Revenue Operations): A little. One retailer acquired a loyalty database, and another ran a reactivation campaign. But the degradation is showing in accounts that did not make a major list change. That is why I am wondering about the shared infrastructure.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): We can investigate that with Deliverability. Are you saying the effect is concentrated among high-volume customers?

Lyla Roob (Revenue Operations): Yes, and that concentration is the scary part. It looks small if you average across the whole customer base, but those few accounts represent a lot of renewal value. One of them is already asking whether they should move their promotional traffic elsewhere.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That is a serious renewal signal. Which account is at the highest risk?

Lyla Roob (Revenue Operations): I would rather not name them in this meeting, but they are a large Benelux retailer with a December renewal. Their marketing director is saying the platform is easy to use and the reporting is strong, but if revenue-driving sends do not reach inboxes, those positives stop mattering.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Understood. Are the smaller-volume accounts seeing any comparable impact?

Lyla Roob (Revenue Operations): A few have normal week-to-week variation. I would not say the issue affects everyone. That is another reason I do not want a blanket message telling customers to change their content. The pattern is more severe among the high-volume group.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): What diagnostics have you already run?

Lyla Roob (Revenue Operations): We checked authentication, suppression handling, bounce trends, and list age. Most of the major accounts have SPF and DKIM configured correctly. We also split one campaign into two batches, and the second batch performed worse despite using the same creative.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That does point toward reputation or infrastructure, although we should avoid jumping to a conclusion from one test.

Lyla Roob (Revenue Operations): Right. We need a controlled comparison. Could ACME tell us which sending pool these accounts use and whether the pool has changed recently?

Priya Shah (Account Executive): I will need to confirm what can be shared, but I can open an escalation and request the relevant pool-level diagnostics.

Lyla Roob (Revenue Operations): Please do that today. We have another promotion scheduled Friday. We may hold it for the highest-volume account, but delaying the send has a cost too.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Would a dedicated IP be an option?

Lyla Roob (Revenue Operations): Possibly, but I do not want to treat dedicated infrastructure as a magic fix. If the customer has weak engagement or an acquired list, they still have work to do. We need to separate those factors.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That is reasonable. What customer guidance would be safe in the meantime?

Lyla Roob (Revenue Operations): Ask them to pause questionable reactivation segments, verify consent, and reduce sudden spikes. But do not tell them the platform is healthy until we understand the shared pool. Some are already nervous.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): I can coordinate a customer-safe message with your team. How should we measure recovery?

Lyla Roob (Revenue Operations): Inbox placement by mailbox provider, complaint rate, hard bounce rate, and revenue per send. Open rate alone is unreliable. We also need to watch renewal milestones for the affected accounts.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Can you provide a weekly list of the top-volume accounts and their renewal dates?

Lyla Roob (Revenue Operations): Yes, through the secure customer channel. It will include ARR bands rather than every financial detail. I also want an incident owner on the ACME side, not a different person every time we ask a question.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): I will request a named owner and a twice-weekly checkpoint until the trend is understood. We may need Support and Deliverability together.

Lyla Roob (Revenue Operations): Good. Also, the Marketo migration comparison is still relevant. We chose ACME partly because the operating model was simpler. If we now need manual monitoring for every major send, the value equation changes.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): I hear that. The QBR should record this as a business risk, not just a technical ticket.

Lyla Roob (Revenue Operations): Exactly. High adoption and good sentiment do not offset a deliverability problem when the affected customers carry disproportionate renewal value.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): I will document the escalation, request the infrastructure analysis, and return with an update before Friday's promotion. Is there anything else?

Lyla Roob (Revenue Operations): One unresolved point: whether the shared pool has a threshold policy that we can explain to customers. If it is shared reputation, we need transparency about how one sender's behavior can affect others.
