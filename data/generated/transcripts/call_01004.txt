Priya Shah (Account Executive): Durward, thanks for meeting. I know your calendar is tight. Before we get into the platform, how is the new healthcare marketing structure settling in?

Durward Mante (CMO): Slowly. We have grown quickly, but marketing still operates as three small teams. The clinical services group, patient education, and corporate partnerships all use different processes.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): What prompted you to explore marketing automation now?

Durward Mante (CMO): We keep losing follow-up. People request information, attend a webinar, or speak with a representative, and then the next step depends on which team received the notification. We need consistency.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Is the issue mainly speed, visibility, or the content itself?

Durward Mante (CMO): Visibility first. I cannot tell whether a lead was contacted, handed to a coordinator, or simply forgotten. Speed matters, but a fast handoff into a black hole is not progress.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): What system holds the contact and activity data today?

Durward Mante (CMO): A CRM, spreadsheets, and a separate patient communications tool. The patient tool is tightly controlled because of privacy requirements. We are not considering replacing it.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Understood. Would the marketing platform work only with non-patient professional contacts?

Durward Mante (CMO): That is the initial assumption, yes. We have referral partners, employers, insurers, and healthcare professionals. I do not want anyone implying that patient records will be imported.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): We can scope the first phase to those audiences and document the boundary. What does a successful handoff look like?

Durward Mante (CMO): A responsible person receives the request, confirms it is relevant, and records the outcome. If they reject it, I want a reason. Right now rejection is silent.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That suggests routing rules and required disposition fields. Do you have agreed criteria for priority?

Durward Mante (CMO): Not really. The partnership team prioritizes large employers. The clinical team prioritizes professional referrals. Corporate wants everything marked urgent.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): We would need to resolve that before automating assignment. Otherwise the system will just formalize the disagreement.

Durward Mante (CMO): Exactly. I am concerned vendors show a beautiful workflow and skip the operating model.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That is a fair concern. We can spend the next stage mapping the process before configuring anything. How many inquiries are you handling monthly?

Durward Mante (CMO): Around one hundred across the teams, though the number is noisy. Some are duplicate webinar registrations, and some are existing partners asking routine questions.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Which program would be safest for a pilot?

Durward Mante (CMO): Partner webinar follow-up. It has a defined audience and a clear owner. Patient education is out of scope, and clinical referrals require a separate review.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): What would you want automated for that pilot?

Durward Mante (CMO): Registration confirmation, attendance follow-up, a reminder for people who did not attend, and a task for the partnership manager when someone requests a meeting.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Would you want different content based on attendance and engagement?

Durward Mante (CMO): Yes, but keep it understandable. Our last consultant produced a twelve-step journey that nobody could explain six weeks later.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): We can start with three paths and document each decision. How should unsubscribes and communication preferences be handled?

Durward Mante (CMO): Centrally. One team can approve messages, but local teams need to see the status. We cannot have someone re-adding a person because their spreadsheet is old.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): We should make the central preference record authoritative and prevent manual imports from overriding it. Is that compatible with your existing tools?

Durward Mante (CMO): I do not know. That is something your technical team must prove. We have had integration promises before.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): We can schedule a technical validation using sample fields and a documented data flow. What would make you reject the proposal?

Durward Mante (CMO): If implementation requires a permanent consultant to operate basic campaigns. Also if pricing assumes every employee will become a user. We probably need twelve active users, not one hundred and eighty-two.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): We can model those user types separately. Would your team be able to dedicate an administrator?

Durward Mante (CMO): Half a day a week, perhaps. More during implementation, but not indefinitely.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That is realistic. We should include training and a governance guide so the system remains maintainable. What reporting do you need at executive level?

Durward Mante (CMO): Number of qualified partner conversations, response time, and conversion to a scheduled meeting. I do not need a chart for every email interaction.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Good. We can keep detailed engagement available for operators but show leadership the three outcomes. Is there a deadline driving the purchase?

Durward Mante (CMO): Our board review is in September. I would like a decision before then, but I will not sign merely to have a logo on a slide.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): What must happen before that decision?

Durward Mante (CMO): Technical validation, privacy review, a pilot design, and a realistic implementation estimate. Finance also needs a total cost, including services.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): I will send a recap with those checkpoints. I would also recommend a short workshop with the three team leads to settle ownership before the demonstration.

Durward Mante (CMO): Yes. If they disagree in the room, that is useful information.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Agreed. Shall we put the workshop on the calendar for next Wednesday?

Durward Mante (CMO): Wednesday morning works. Send the questions in advance so people do not arrive prepared only to defend their current spreadsheet.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): I will. Thank you, Durward.

Durward Mante (CMO): Thanks. I am interested, but the process and privacy boundaries are more important than feature count.
