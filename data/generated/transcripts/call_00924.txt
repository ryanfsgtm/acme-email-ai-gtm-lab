Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Fabiola, thanks for making time. I understand the previous opportunity is closed-lost, so today I’d like to understand the evaluation from the implementation side.

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): That works. I was involved in the technical review, but the final decision came from a wider committee.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): What problem were you trying to solve?

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): We have a large distributed organisation. Each region runs campaigns differently, and central IT gets pulled into every integration. The initial objective was consistency without forcing every team into the same content.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): How did you handle campaigns before the evaluation?

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): Regional teams used local tools, spreadsheets, and some internal scripts. Data travelled through nightly jobs. It was not elegant, but people knew the quirks.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): What did the proposed platform demonstrate well?

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): The journey canvas was clear. It showed entry criteria, waits, conditions, and handoffs. Non-technical users understood it faster than they understood our scripts.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): And where did confidence fall?

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): Integration ownership. Everyone assumed someone else would maintain the connectors. The vendor described a standard setup, but our environment has multiple identity systems and regional consent stores.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Did the team test those variations?

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): We tested one region. That worked. We did not complete the edge cases for bounced addresses, withdrawn consent, or contacts shared between business units.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Those edge cases can change the delivery design substantially.

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): Yes. The demo made it look like a single global model. Our real model is a federation with exceptions everywhere.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): What were the security concerns?

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): Access boundaries, audit logs, and deletion. A regional administrator should not see another region’s personal data. We also needed evidence that a deleted contact was removed from downstream processing.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Did the documentation answer that?

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): It answered the general architecture, not our exact flow. That distinction mattered to the review board.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): What about user adoption?

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): Marketing liked the interface, but operations worried about governance. If every local team creates its own naming scheme, reporting becomes unreliable. If central IT approves every change, the system becomes slow.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Did you identify a compromise?

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): We proposed global templates with local content slots. But we never agreed who owned the templates or how often they could change.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): What role did reporting play?

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): Executives wanted a consolidated view, naturally. But the numbers were not comparable because regions define conversion differently. A global dashboard would have created an illusion of precision.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Would standard definitions have solved that?

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): Eventually, yes, but that is a transformation project, not a configuration detail. We had underestimated it.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): How did the commercial review affect the outcome?

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): The price was significant, but price was not the sole issue. We could not support the staffing required for rollout. The opportunity would have needed regional champions, data owners, and a central administrator.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Were those resources unavailable permanently?

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): No, but they were committed to another programme through the year. The committee did not want to buy capability that would sit idle.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): What would a better implementation plan have looked like?

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): A small region, one use case, and a hard boundary around data. Prove the operating model before scaling. We tried to design the end state first.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Was there any functionality you considered unnecessary?

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): The more advanced personalisation was impressive, but not urgent. We needed dependable consent handling and basic routing. Sophistication was not the blocker, even though it dominated some conversations.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Did the team have a preferred alternative?

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): We decided to extend internal tooling temporarily. It is less polished, but the owners are known and the change process is familiar.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): That makes sense as a risk decision. What should future evaluations clarify early?

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): Data ownership, deletion semantics, regional boundaries, and the support model. Also ask who wakes up at two in the morning when an integration fails. That question was missing.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): It rarely appears on a feature checklist, but it should.

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): Exactly. We also needed a realistic demonstration with bad data. Clean sample records made the process look easier than it is.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Did the previous team communicate that the opportunity was lost because of product limitations?

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): They described it as timing and internal prioritisation. That is partly true. But the evaluation also exposed gaps in our own governance, so it would be unfair to blame the product alone.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): That nuance is useful. Is there a future path?

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): Possibly after the identity and consent programmes finish. We would want a technical workshop first, then a limited pilot. No broad purchase based on a polished demo.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): I’ll capture that as a conditional re-entry path, not an active opportunity.

Fabiola Gutmann (Implementation Partner): Good. And please send the technical questions back to the product team. Even though we didn’t proceed, the answers would improve the next review.
