Priya Shah (Account Executive): King, thanks for making time. Before we get into screens or features, could you give me the operational picture from your side?

King Steuber (Sales Operations): Sure. I support sales operations across a healthcare organization with a little over twenty-one thousand employees, although the group that would initially use a marketing automation system is much smaller. We have centralized governance and fairly independent regional teams.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): When you say independent, do they run different campaigns or just execute centrally defined ones?

King Steuber (Sales Operations): Both. Corporate produces a lot of the core material, but regional teams adapt it for local providers and referral networks. The approval paths differ, and data access is not uniform. A campaign can be approved in Toronto while the local team is still checking whether the wording is permitted in its market.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): What is prompting the evaluation now?

King Steuber (Sales Operations): We have too many disconnected handoffs. Lead lists come from several systems, campaign engagement is in another place, and sales activity is not consistently returned to marketing. People can produce reports, but they are stitching them together manually.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): How often do those reports need to be produced?

King Steuber (Sales Operations): Weekly for operating reviews, monthly for leadership, and ad hoc when a regional president asks why a referral campaign did not convert. The numbers are never wildly different, but they are different enough to trigger an investigation.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Is the primary goal better campaign execution, better visibility, or the connection between marketing activity and sales outcomes?

King Steuber (Sales Operations): The connection. Campaign execution is imperfect, but we can live with some imperfection if we understand what happened. Right now, we cannot reliably tell whether a qualified referral came from an email journey, an event, a field representative, or a combination.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): How are leads currently qualified and routed?

King Steuber (Sales Operations): It depends on the business line. Some use a score. Some use a form plus a manual review. A few regional groups send everything to an operations queue because they do not trust the scoring. That creates delay, but at least nobody claims the model is making a clinical decision.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): The healthcare context makes that caution understandable. What would a safe first phase look like?

King Steuber (Sales Operations): A bounded pilot with one corporate program and two regions. It would need clear data permissions, auditability, and a way to stop or amend a journey without waiting for a central administrator in another time zone.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Who would own the pilot?

King Steuber (Sales Operations): I would coordinate it, but marketing operations would own the campaigns. IT security, privacy, and the regional compliance leads all have review rights. Procurement will want a commercial proposal only after the security path is credible.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): What systems would be in scope for integration?

King Steuber (Sales Operations): Our CRM, the event platform, and a data warehouse. There are also provider directories, but I would keep those out of phase one. We need to avoid turning a pilot into an enterprise architecture program.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That is a useful boundary. What CRM data is trustworthy today?

King Steuber (Sales Operations): Account ownership and opportunity stages are reasonably good. Contact role data is less reliable. We have duplicates, old job titles, and records that belong to a network rather than a single facility.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Would matching and deduplication be part of the evaluation criteria?

King Steuber (Sales Operations): Yes, but with human review. A false merge could hide an important relationship. I would rather tolerate some duplicates than combine entities incorrectly.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): What reporting would the steering group expect?

King Steuber (Sales Operations): Campaign performance by region, engagement by audience, handoff time, and sourced or influenced opportunity value. They will also ask for an exception report: records blocked because consent, ownership, or required fields are missing.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): The exception report is often where operational trust is won. Do you have target thresholds in mind?

King Steuber (Sales Operations): Directionally. We want handoff visibility within a business day, and we want regional teams to explain exceptions without opening a ticket for every one. I am not promising a specific percentage yet.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Understood. How will success be decided?

King Steuber (Sales Operations): We will compare the pilot against the current process. Not just clicks and opens. I want time to launch, time to route, report reconciliation effort, and whether sales managers can understand the history without asking an analyst.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Who else should be in the next conversation?

King Steuber (Sales Operations): Marketing operations, someone from privacy, and one regional campaign manager. Sales leadership can join later. If we invite everyone now, the meeting will become a debate about the entire operating model.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): What is the likely timeline?

King Steuber (Sales Operations): Discovery and security review this quarter. If those are clean, a pilot could begin in the next planning window. The opportunity record may show a July close date, but that is a planning placeholder, not a commitment.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Thanks for clarifying. I will summarize the pilot boundaries, required controls, integration assumptions, and measurement plan. I will also identify which claims need confirmation from our technical team.

King Steuber (Sales Operations): Please do. I am interested, but I do not want a polished demo to outrun the governance work.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That is fair. Would you prefer a technical workshop before a product demo?

King Steuber (Sales Operations): A short technical workshop first. Then show only the workflows relevant to the pilot. If the system can support those cleanly, we can broaden the discussion.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): I will schedule that and include the data-permission questions in advance.

King Steuber (Sales Operations): Good. Also include a sample exception report. That will tell us more than another dashboard full of green arrows.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Agreed. Thanks, King.

King Steuber (Sales Operations): Thank you, Priya. I have to jump to an operating review.
