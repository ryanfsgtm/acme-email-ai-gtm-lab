Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Rose, thanks for taking the time. I understand this is a technical validation for a closed-lost opportunity, with Eloqua listed as the selected competitor. I would like to confirm a few requirements and identify anything we missed during the evaluation.

Rose Halvorson (Sales Operations): Yes, that is right. We are a 34-person manufacturing company in France. The sales team is small, and we decided Eloqua was the safer choice for the current project. We have not started a full rollout yet.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): What does “safer” mean for your team?

Rose Halvorson (Sales Operations): It is familiar to our parent organisation, and our consultant already supports it. That matters. We do not have an internal marketing operations person, so outside help reduces the burden.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Is the main requirement lead routing, campaign execution, or reporting?

Rose Halvorson (Sales Operations): Lead routing and visibility. Marketing is one person plus me part-time. We need to know when someone requests a quote, whether sales followed up, and which enquiries are still untouched. Campaign sophistication is secondary.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): How are quote requests handled today?

Rose Halvorson (Sales Operations): A web form sends an email to a shared mailbox. Someone copies the information into the CRM. If the person is already known, we try to match the record. If they are not, the data may sit in the mailbox until the next morning.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Would immediate creation and assignment in the CRM solve most of the problem?

Rose Halvorson (Sales Operations): It would solve a lot, but not all. We have distributors, existing customers, and new prospects using similar forms. A rule that sends every request to a salesperson could create noise.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): So routing needs context, not just a round-robin assignment.

Rose Halvorson (Sales Operations): Exactly. Country, product family, customer status, and urgency all matter. Product family is sometimes entered inconsistently, which is another issue.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Could we start by standardising the form choices and using a small routing matrix?

Rose Halvorson (Sales Operations): Probably. But our website agency controls the form, and changes require a ticket. That dependency is part of the reason we prefer a managed implementation.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): What reporting do managers ask for?

Rose Halvorson (Sales Operations): Open enquiries by owner, time to first response, quote conversion, and source. They do not need a huge attribution model. They need a weekly view that does not require me to reconcile three spreadsheets.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): How accurate is your source data today?

Rose Halvorson (Sales Operations): Not very. Source is often blank, and salespeople choose “website” even when the contact came from a distributor. I do not want a platform judged on attribution numbers that our process cannot support.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): That is fair. We should distinguish process improvements from historical data quality. What would be the minimum viable integration?

Rose Halvorson (Sales Operations): Web form to CRM, assignment, acknowledgement email, and a dashboard. We would need logs so I can see whether a submission failed. We cannot discover a failure because a customer complains three days later.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): We can show integration logs and alerts. Do you need two-way synchronisation with the ERP?

Rose Halvorson (Sales Operations): Not initially. The ERP has account and order information, but connecting it now would expand the project. Sales can check the ERP manually for existing customers during phase one.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): That is a sensible boundary. What security or hosting questions will your parent company ask?

Rose Halvorson (Sales Operations): Data location, access controls, retention, and whether administrators can export contacts. They may also ask whether the vendor has a French support option, although English is fine for me.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): We can provide the security documentation and clarify support coverage. Were there any concerns about our product during the evaluation?

Rose Halvorson (Sales Operations): The interface looked capable, but we worried it might be too much for us. The demo showed advanced journeys when our immediate need was a queue and a few dependable rules. Eloqua felt like an established path because the parent company knows it.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): That is useful feedback. A smaller implementation can still use a broader platform, but the first design should not expose unnecessary complexity.

Rose Halvorson (Sales Operations): Right. Also, our expected volume is modest. Maybe 200 web enquiries a month, with seasonal variation. We do not need an enterprise architecture for its own sake.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Would a proof with ten anonymised examples be enough to validate routing?

Rose Halvorson (Sales Operations): Yes, if the examples include an existing customer, a distributor, a French prospect, an urgent request, and a malformed product selection. The ugly cases are more informative than the perfect ones.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): I like that test set. Who would approve the result?

Rose Halvorson (Sales Operations): Our sales director and the parent-company operations lead. Finance also needs the total cost, including services. The decision is not reopening today, but a failed Eloqua rollout could lead to another review.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): When is Eloqua implementation expected to begin?

Rose Halvorson (Sales Operations): Within the next month, subject to the consultant’s availability. If it works cleanly, we will stay with it. If the project becomes too heavy for our team, we may reconsider.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Then the most useful next step is a short technical note, not a generic follow-up. I will document the minimum architecture, assumptions, logs, and test cases. You can keep it for comparison.

Rose Halvorson (Sales Operations): Please do. Make clear that our main concern is maintainability. We do not want a system that only the consultant understands.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Agreed. We will include ownership and a simple admin checklist.

Rose Halvorson (Sales Operations): Good. I appreciate that you are not trying to turn a small operation into a giant programme.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): The right-sized solution is usually the better technical solution. Thanks, Rose.

Rose Halvorson (Sales Operations): Thank you, Nina. Send the note this week and I will share it internally.
