Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Fair distinction. We can document the classification and have legal or privacy approval for each program. What data should never be copied into the marketing platform?

Cornell Lockman (Privacy): Sensitive student details, accommodation information, disciplinary notes, and anything collected solely for admissions assessment. We should not import a field simply because it exists.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): We will create an allowlist rather than a broad export. Who approves it?

Cornell Lockman (Privacy): No. The fundraising team should not automatically see admissions notes, and student services should not inherit donor segmentation. The organization is small enough that people assume they can see everything because they know each other.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): We can use role-based access and limit exports. Do you need a review of the audit trail?

Cornell Lockman (Privacy): Absolutely. We need to know who changed consent, who exported a list, and when a suppression was removed. The audit trail must be useful to an investigator, not merely available somewhere.
