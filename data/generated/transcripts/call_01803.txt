Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): So the handoff from marketing activity into revenue work is the gap.

Flavio Fritsch (Revenue Operations): Yes, although “revenue” sounds grand for an education nonprofit. We have course enrollments, institutional partnerships, grants, and alumni engagement. The same contact can appear in all four contexts.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): That makes identity and segmentation important. How are contacts managed today?

Flavio Fritsch (Revenue Operations): Spreadsheets, a CRM, and a form tool that was chosen by someone who has since left. The CRM is technically the source of truth, but the team exports to spreadsheets whenever a campaign needs a special audience.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): How often are those exports refreshed?

Flavio Fritsch (Revenue Operations): Depends on the campaign. Sometimes daily for an enrollment push. Sometimes not at all. There is a tab called “current” that is definitely not current.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): That sounds familiar. What would you want an automation platform to do first?

Flavio Fritsch (Revenue Operations): Capture forms, deduplicate people, route the right follow-up, and show me whether a campaign generated a qualified institutional conversation. We also need German-language emails. English-only would be a nonstarter.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Would the same person receive both institutional and student communications?

Flavio Fritsch (Revenue Operations): Potentially, but not at the same time and not based on a single generic newsletter preference. Someone can be an alum and work for a school district. Consent needs to be understandable to the person and usable by our team.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): How is consent recorded today?

Flavio Fritsch (Revenue Operations): A checkbox on some forms, a manual note on older records, and an unsubscribe file from the email tool. It is not elegant. We have never had a serious complaint, but I would rather not rely on that luck.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): We could map source, timestamp, purpose, and status, but older records may need a treatment decision. Would you suppress them, re-permission them, or leave them outside the first campaign?

Flavio Fritsch (Revenue Operations): Probably leave them outside until we agree. I do not want a migration project to become a consent interpretation project.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Good boundary. Let’s talk about routing. What happens after an institutional form is submitted?

Flavio Fritsch (Revenue Operations): An email goes to a shared inbox. Someone reads it and forwards it to the person who looks least busy. We have a service-level goal of two business days, but no system tells me whether we met it.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Would assignment be based on region, organization type, or program?

Flavio Fritsch (Revenue Operations): Program first, then region. But there are only four people who can handle these conversations, so a round-robin fallback would help when someone is away.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): And what counts as qualified?

Flavio Fritsch (Revenue Operations): A real organization, a defined learner or staff need, an approximate timeline, and a person willing to schedule. We can capture the first two in a form. The last two usually emerge later.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Then scoring should probably be advisory rather than an automatic qualification gate.

Flavio Fritsch (Revenue Operations): Exactly. I am wary of a number becoming a decision nobody can explain.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): What reporting do your leaders ask for?

Flavio Fritsch (Revenue Operations): Enrollment campaign response, partner pipeline, event follow-up, and conversion by source. They also ask for a single funnel chart, even though the journeys are not really one funnel.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): That is a common tension. We can provide a shared activity view while preserving separate lifecycle definitions. Do you have a target for implementation?

Flavio Fritsch (Revenue Operations): Before the autumn enrollment cycle. The date is flexible in theory and absolutely not flexible once brochures are printed.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): What resources can you provide?
