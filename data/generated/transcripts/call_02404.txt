Marcus Chen (Account Executive): We can define a restricted data model and map only the fields required for segmentation and communication. The CRM remains authoritative for customer and account data. Would pseudonymous identifiers be preferable for some behavioral events?

Kyle Hoppe (Security): Possibly. I need the data-flow diagram first. “We can” is not a control until I see the fields, endpoints, and retention behavior.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): Fair. I will have our technical team provide the diagram and a field-level example. For identity, are you expecting SSO through your corporate provider?

Kyle Hoppe (Security): Yes, for users. MFA must be enforced through our identity provider, and administrator roles need separation from campaign authoring roles. A marketer should not be able to grant themselves privileged access.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): We support SSO and role-based permissions. We can outline standard roles and map them to your approval model. Do you require just-in-time provisioning or a managed user list?

Kyle Hoppe (Security): We prefer managed provisioning. If you support SCIM, include it in the response, but do not make it a hidden prerequisite for the basic deployment.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): Understood. We will distinguish available controls from optional services. How do you assess auditability?

Kyle Hoppe (Security): We need administrator activity logs, authentication events, configuration changes, and export capability. Logs should be retained long enough for our investigation process. I also need to know whether logs can be sent to our monitoring platform.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): We can document the log types and delivery options. I will verify the exact retention period and whether the integration is native or requires an intermediary.

Kyle Hoppe (Security): Please verify rather than saying “near real time.” That phrase has caused arguments.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): Noted. On encryption, we use encryption in transit and at rest as standard. Are there customer-managed key requirements?

Kyle Hoppe (Security): Not for this workload, unless the risk assessment changes. We do need current independent assurance reports, vulnerability-management information, and a clear incident-notification commitment.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): We can provide the relevant reports under the appropriate confidentiality process. For incident notification, our legal and security teams can answer the contractual language. Would your vendor-risk office handle the formal review?

Kyle Hoppe (Security): Yes. I am an assessor and technical gatekeeper, not the commercial approver. Procurement will need the pricing and terms separately.
