Hattie Johnston (Lifecycle Marketing): Data freshness. Some media engagement events arrive late, especially from one partner feed. The journey can’t react to an event it hasn’t received.
Luis Romero (Customer Success Manager): Is the delay consistent or intermittent?
Hattie Johnston (Lifecycle Marketing): Intermittent. On a normal day it’s under an hour. During a campaign spike, we’ve seen several hours. Our team initially blamed the platform, but the source export was late too.
Luis Romero (Customer Success Manager): Good catch. We can still monitor it, but we should keep the source dependency visible. I’ll add a data-latency alert to the action list.
Hattie Johnston (Lifecycle Marketing): Please do. Another issue is reporting. The campaign-level numbers are clear, but the revenue view takes explanation. Finance asks why the dashboard doesn’t match their month-end report.
Luis Romero (Customer Success Manager): That’s usually a windowing or attribution-model difference. Have you agreed on the reporting definition with finance?
Hattie Johnston (Lifecycle Marketing): Not fully. Marketing is looking at influenced revenue, while finance prefers booked revenue by invoice date. The dashboard is not necessarily wrong; we’re answering different questions.
Luis Romero (Customer Success Manager): Exactly. We can create two saved views with explicit labels, so the disagreement is visible rather than hidden in a single number.
Hattie Johnston (Lifecycle Marketing): I’d like that. Leadership sees one screenshot and assumes it is the official answer.

Luis Romero (Customer Success Manager): Would additional enablement help them?
Hattie Johnston (Lifecycle Marketing): A short session on interpreting the reports would help more than a product tour. People know where to click; they don’t always know what a conversion window means.

Hattie Johnston (Lifecycle Marketing): That would be better than another generic training deck.
