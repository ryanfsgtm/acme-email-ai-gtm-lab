Marcus Chen (Account Executive): Then we will keep this one predictable. I wanted to review adoption, the migration work, and the next quarter’s priorities. You moved from Marketo, correct?

Ottis Kutch (Security): Correct. The migration looked risky in aggregate. There were years of programs, custom fields, and assumptions nobody had written down.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): That is a fair description. How did the actual launch compare with the initial concern?

Ottis Kutch (Security): Better than expected, but not because the platform magically made the work simple. Our certified implementation partner forced a proper inventory, mapped the fields, and staged the launch. That reduced the uncertainty.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): So the partner involvement changed the risk profile?

Ottis Kutch (Security): Yes. They knew where Marketo migrations usually break. They did not promise that every asset could be copied. They made us decide what to retire.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): And the timing?

Ottis Kutch (Security): We launched the first production journeys ahead of the revised plan. The original plan was too optimistic, though, so I would not call it fast in an absolute sense.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): Fair. Which security controls are now routine?

Ottis Kutch (Security): SSO, role reviews, audit-log checks, and approval on production changes. We still have a quarterly access review, but the evidence is easier to collect.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): Are there any permissions you want changed?

Ottis Kutch (Security): We need to revisit export permissions. Marketing wants broad access for analysis. Security wants a smaller group able to extract contact data. We have not resolved the balance.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): We can review role-based alternatives and perhaps separate reporting access from raw-data export. Would that help?

Ottis Kutch (Security): Probably. I do not want a blanket restriction that makes the business invent workarounds.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): How are the lifecycle teams using the system?

Ottis Kutch (Security): A sandbox or a safe preview workflow. People need to see the audience and suppression logic before activation.

Ottis Kutch (Security): We track launch cycle time, error tickets, and access exceptions. Marketing tracks engagement, but I do not want engagement to become the only definition of success.

Ottis Kutch (Security): Yes, after the first month. Some early tickets were not product defects; they were old Marketo assumptions carried into the new model.
