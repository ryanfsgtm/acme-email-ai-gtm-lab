Priya Shah (Account Executive): Otto, good to see you. How are things at Goldner Group?

Otto Hane (Demand Generation): Busy, but in the ordinary way. We’re a small team, so ordinary busy still looks like three emergencies and one person making coffee for everyone.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): I know the feeling. I wanted to use today to review the renewal, hear how the programs are performing, and understand whether expansion is still something you want to consider.

Otto Hane (Demand Generation): Renewal is the immediate question. We have about fifty-one employees and four people touching demand generation. We don’t have room for a platform that needs a specialist every morning.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): How has the current setup been working?

Otto Hane (Demand Generation): Better for email than for the broader automation vision. We can build campaigns. Reporting is less convincing, especially when sales changes the CRM stages after the fact.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Has your executive sponsor been involved recently?

Otto Hane (Demand Generation): Not recently, no. Our former COO sponsored the project, then left. The interim COO has been focused on a distribution partnership and hasn’t attended the last few meetings.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That’s helpful to know. How are decisions being made now?

Otto Hane (Demand Generation): Mostly tactically. I speak with the marketing manager about emails and forms. Sales operations answers questions when they can. We haven’t had an executive conversation about the platform in months.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): What would need to happen for renewal to move forward?

Otto Hane (Account Executive): We need a clear cost case and someone senior to say the system remains a priority. The date has moved twice because we keep waiting for that conversation.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): The current target is the end of this month. Is that still realistic?

Otto Hane (Demand Generation): I wouldn’t put money on it. We may shift it again. The team is still asking whether we renew the existing package or simplify the stack.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Understood. What would a simplified stack mean for you?

Otto Hane (Demand Generation): Fewer campaigns, fewer integrations, and probably no expansion. We could keep the basic forms and newsletters while deciding what the next year looks like.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Could we schedule a short value review with the interim COO and your sales operations lead?

Otto Hane (Demand Generation): I can ask. I can’t say they’ll attend. The COO may send someone from finance instead.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Finance could still be useful if we bring usage, campaign contribution, and renewal options. What numbers would they trust?

Otto Hane (Demand Generation): Cost per qualified inquiry, influenced pipeline, and hours saved. But the pipeline number is messy. We’ve had two good campaigns that sales followed up quickly, and several where the leads sat untouched.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): So the platform’s performance is mixed with process performance.

Otto Hane (Demand Generation): Exactly. I don’t want to blame the tool for sales ignoring a lead, but I also can’t present influenced pipeline as if the handoff was perfect.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That distinction belongs in the review. Would you be comfortable documenting the operational gaps alongside the results?

Otto Hane (Demand Generation): Yes, if it doesn’t turn into an excuse to sell us more modules. We’re not ready for a big expansion conversation.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): I’ll keep the scope to renewal and a small set of options. What is the next internal milestone?

Otto Hane (Demand Generation): I have a team meeting Friday. We’ll decide whether to recommend renewal, pause, or a reduced footprint. Then it goes to the interim COO, whenever her calendar opens.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): I’ll send a one-page summary before Friday with the current usage, the unresolved adoption questions, and three commercial paths. If you can add comments after the team meeting, we can decide whether an executive review is worthwhile.

Otto Hane (Demand Generation): That works. Please include the close-date history; people keep assuming the date is firm because it is in the CRM.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): I will. And I’ll note that the prior sponsor is no longer in role, so the decision path needs to be rebuilt.

Otto Hane (Demand Generation): Good. I’m not saying no, but there isn’t a champion with authority right now. Until someone owns the decision, we’ll keep having practical calls about individual campaigns.
