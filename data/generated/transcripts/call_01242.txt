Alessia Lockman (VP Marketing): Both, depending on the service line. Oncology is extremely cautious. General wellness is faster. If someone presents a single average, it will be misleading.

Alessia Lockman (VP Marketing): We track qualified referrals, appointment requests, engagement, and education content consumption. Leadership wants a connection to service-line growth, but we are careful about claiming direct causality.

Alessia Lockman (VP Marketing): The finance team trusts the pipeline report. Marketing trusts the campaign report. Those are not always the same report. And compliance wants to know who changed a message after approval.

Alessia Lockman (VP Marketing): Correct. We had an incident where a footer changed between review and send. It was fixed quickly, but now every group asks for stronger controls.

Alessia Lockman (VP Marketing): The operating model. We may be over-centralized. But I need evidence that local teams can move faster without creating compliance exceptions.
