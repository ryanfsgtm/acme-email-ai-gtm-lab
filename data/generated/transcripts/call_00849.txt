Annabelle Zieme (Privacy): Agencies and multi-brand companies need isolated workspaces, reusable templates, and consolidated reporting. We manage client campaigns with different permissions and retention expectations. If everything sits in one workspace, an accidental selection becomes too easy.

Annabelle Zieme (Privacy): Four, possibly five. They should not all share the same user access. At the same time, leadership wants one view of performance and compliance status across the practice.

Annabelle Zieme (Privacy): Please do. Also, reusable templates need review status. A template approved for one client may not be acceptable for another, especially where notices or retention periods differ.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): We can treat templates as centrally managed assets with workspace-level availability. I will confirm whether approval metadata can be enforced or only documented.

Annabelle Zieme (Privacy): That distinction matters. Documentation is helpful, but enforcement is what prevents a tired coordinator from choosing the wrong version on a Friday afternoon.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): Understood. On reporting, would leadership need campaign metrics, compliance exceptions, or both?

Annabelle Zieme (Privacy): Both, but not necessarily in the same report. Executives want consolidated performance. Privacy wants exceptions, access changes, and evidence that consent rules were applied.
