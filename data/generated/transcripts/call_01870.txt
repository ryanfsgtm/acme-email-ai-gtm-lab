Duane Goodwin (VP Marketing): That was the original outcome. Since then, the board has asked us to slow down spending. We lost the opportunity in April, and the budget is not approved for a restart yet.

Duane Goodwin (VP Marketing): Correct. I do not want the team pretending we are two weeks from go-live. We need a realistic plan and a view of what we could do later.

Duane Goodwin (VP Marketing): Website forms, newsletter sends, a simple lead-routing process, and reporting by service line. We also wanted to stop the weekly CSV dance between marketing and sales.

Luis Romero (Customer Success Manager): What is the current workflow for a new inquiry?

Duane Goodwin (VP Marketing): Someone receives an email notification, copies the details into a spreadsheet, checks whether the company is already known, and forwards it to a partner. Sometimes that happens the same day. Sometimes it sits until Monday.

Luis Romero (Customer Success Manager): What determines priority?

Duane Goodwin (VP Marketing): Service line, location, and whether the inquiry mentions a current client. We have discussed scoring, but the partners do not agree on what a “hot” lead means.

Luis Romero (Customer Success Manager): Then scoring should not be the first build item. We could document routing rules and agree on ownership before adding a score.

Duane Goodwin (VP Marketing): Yes. The sales team tends to ask for sophisticated automation when the basic ownership table is still in a drawer somewhere.

Luis Romero (Customer Success Manager): For data, what records would be migrated?

Duane Goodwin (VP Marketing): Around 11,000 contacts in total, though many are old. We have clients, prospects, alumni from past events, and personal addresses that should probably never have been imported.

Luis Romero (Customer Success Manager): We would need a classification exercise, consent review, and duplicate handling before any send. Who owns those decisions?

Duane Goodwin (VP Marketing): Marketing can classify the business purpose. Our external counsel should advise on consent and retention. I will not have an intern decide whether an old contact can be mailed.

Luis Romero (Customer Success Manager): Good. What would a small pilot look like if funding returns?

Duane Goodwin (VP Marketing): One service line, one form, one follow-up sequence, and a report the partners actually read. Maybe 1,500 contacts, not the entire database.

Luis Romero (Customer Success Manager): That is a sensible pilot. We can define success as form capture, correct owner assignment, response-time visibility, and approved reporting.

Duane Goodwin (VP Marketing): Add content approval. Our subject-matter experts change wording at the last minute, and the approval trail matters.

Luis Romero (Customer Success Manager): Noted. We can include draft, review, approval, and scheduled states. Would you want partners approving every email?

Duane Goodwin (VP Marketing): Exactly. Partners need a short view of what changed and where to see inquiries. They will not attend a product tour.
