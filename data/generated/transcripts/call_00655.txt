Randi Mosciski-Bayer (Implementation Partner): Delivery and unsubscribe rates first, then qualified replies and accepted handoffs. I would not promise pipeline in the first two weeks. The audience is small, and one conversation can matter more than a percentage.

Randi Mosciski-Bayer (Implementation Partner): An explicit request for a meeting or information should create a task. A simple open should not. We can use a manual review queue for ambiguous replies until the team learns the pattern.
