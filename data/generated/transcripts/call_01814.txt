Amelia Hart (Account Executive): Abraham, good to see you again. How’s the new quarter treating you?

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): Congratulations, or condolences—possibly both. I wanted to review the current marketing automation evaluation and talk about whether an expansion makes sense for Bradtke - Emard. Your existing footprint is modest, and the opportunity is still open at 14,070. Has anything changed since our last conversation?

Abraham Johns (VP Marketing): The business is growing, but I’m not ready to treat growth as permission to buy every feature. The immediate question is whether the platform helps us publish consistently without creating more administration.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): What does “consistently” mean for your team?

Abraham Johns (VP Marketing): Two campaigns a month, a reliable newsletter, and follow-up for product inquiries. Right now the newsletter is easy enough. The follow-up is where we lose people because I’m switching between forms, inboxes, and the CRM.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): Are you looking for more automation, or better visibility into the current process?

Abraham Johns (VP Marketing): Both, but visibility first. If an automated sequence sends the wrong message, I need to know quickly. We sell software to small businesses, and a sloppy email feels personal even when the list is small.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): That’s a good reason to keep the audience controlled. How do you segment today?

Abraham Johns (VP Marketing): Mostly by product interest and whether they’re an existing customer. We don’t have enough volume for elaborate scoring. I don’t want a model pretending it knows more than we do.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): We could start with a few explicit rules and add scoring later. Would expansion mean more contacts, additional users, or new campaigns?

Abraham Johns (VP Marketing): Probably two additional users and the forms integration. Maybe a customer onboarding sequence. But I need to understand the cost and the time required. I can’t spend six weeks configuring a system to save three hours a week.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): Fair. A basic expansion could be implemented in a few working sessions. The onboarding sequence would require approved content and a clear handoff to support. Do you have that content already?

Abraham Johns (VP Marketing): We have rough emails, not approved messaging. Also, our support lead thinks onboarding belongs in the help desk. I don’t want to create a second source of truth.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): Then we should treat ownership as part of the decision. The automation can send reminders, but support should own the customer-facing guidance if that’s their policy.

Abraham Johns (VP Marketing): Exactly. I’ve seen demos where the answer is “automate it,” and then nobody asks who maintains it.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): Let me ask about the renewal side. Are you satisfied with the current service and deliverability?

Abraham Johns (VP Marketing): Deliverability has been fine. We had one unsubscribe spike after a partner webinar, but that was our targeting mistake. I don’t want to blame the system for that.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): What would you need to see before approving expansion?

Abraham Johns (VP Marketing): A short pilot or at least a working session using our actual form. I want to see the contact created correctly, the consent field retained, and the task assigned to the right person. Then show me how I pause a campaign when something changes.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): We can do that. Do you have a target date?

Abraham Johns (VP Marketing): I’d like to decide before the end of November, but the opportunity is still in evaluation. December is our busiest sales month, so implementation before then would be a mistake.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): That suggests a decision this quarter with activation in January.

Abraham Johns (VP Marketing): Maybe. The board also wants evidence that marketing is contributing to pipeline, and I’m not sure our current reporting can support that claim.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): We can define a small baseline: source, response, qualified inquiry, and accepted opportunity. It won’t prove everything, but it can establish a consistent view.

Abraham Johns (VP Marketing): That would help. Please don’t promise attribution beyond what the data can support.

Amelia Hart (Account Executive): Agreed. For pricing, I’ll prepare an option for the current renewal plus two users and forms integration, and a second option including onboarding automation. No assumption that you choose the larger package.

Abraham Johns (VP Marketing): Good. I also want annual and monthly cash-flow views. We’re an SMB, and timing matters more than a discount headline.
