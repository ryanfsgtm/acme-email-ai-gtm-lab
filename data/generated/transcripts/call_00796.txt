Marcus Chen (Account Executive): That's fair feedback. Who attended from your side?

April Cremin (Procurement): A quantified baseline. Current lead-to-opportunity conversion, campaign-influenced pipeline, the time spent building lists and handoffs—those numbers. Also, a phased plan. April Cremin (Procurement): Possibly, but I wouldn't want another generic workshop. We have had enough of those. If it produced a one-page business case with assumptions clearly labelled, that could travel better internally.

April Cremin (Procurement): Finance business partnering would be the right group. Our marketing director is supportive, but she won't approve a project that she cannot defend to the board. And the board is looking closely at operating costs because orders have been uneven.
