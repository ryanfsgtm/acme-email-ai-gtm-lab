Ofelia West (VP Marketing): The marketing team liked the platform. The decision came down to risk tolerance and timing. Our security review found several answers that were acceptable technically but too slow to resolve commercially.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Which answers caused the most friction?

Ofelia West (VP Marketing): Data residency, administrator access, and deletion from backups. The written responses were careful, but they did not give our privacy counsel the exact commitments she wanted.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Did the absence of a specific data-residency option make the difference, or was it the review duration?

Ofelia West (VP Marketing): Both, but mostly timing. We had an internal launch date. When the review extended, finance would not hold the budget open indefinitely.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That is helpful. Was there a competing vendor with a cleaner security package?

Ofelia West (VP Marketing): We did not select another automation platform immediately. Some teams are continuing with existing tools while we revisit the programme later. That is why I do not want the record to say we lost solely to a competitor.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Understood. Did the existing tools avoid the same privacy questions, or were they simply already approved?

Ofelia West (VP Marketing): Already approved. That does not mean they are better. It means the internal effort to use them was lower, even though the capabilities are weaker.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That distinction matters. On administrator access, were you concerned about our support personnel, your own administrators, or both?

Ofelia West (VP Marketing): Both. Security wanted a clear process for emergency support access, including approval, logging, and expiry. Your pack described the controls, but the legal wording did not match the operational description closely enough.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): So the concern was alignment between documents rather than a confirmed control failure.

Ofelia West (VP Marketing): Correct. We could not get comfortable with ambiguity under the deadline.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): For deletion from backups, did your team require immediate deletion, or a documented schedule?

Ofelia West (VP Marketing): A documented schedule with a maximum period. Our counsel asked twice and received two slightly different explanations. That undermined confidence.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): I’m sorry; inconsistent wording is avoidable. If we re-enter the evaluation, would a single technical-legal response pack help?

Ofelia West (VP Marketing): Yes, if it is prepared before the formal review. We would need the DPA, subprocessor list, residency statement, support-access procedure, and backup-retention schedule in one controlled version.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): And who would need to review it internally?

Ofelia West (VP Marketing): Privacy counsel, information security, procurement, and finance. Marketing can sponsor the use case, but we cannot waive their requirements.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Were there any business objections to the product itself?

Ofelia West (VP Marketing): Not really. The implementation estimate felt ambitious for our regional teams, but that was secondary. The deciding issue was whether we could complete approval before the budget cycle closed.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That suggests a future process might begin with security and legal discovery before a full commercial proposal.

Ofelia West (VP Marketing): Exactly. If you wait until the end, the technical questions become a schedule risk. Also, please avoid assuming that every enterprise healthcare account needs the same deployment. Our first phase would be limited to two regions.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That is a useful correction. A narrower initial scope could reduce data and access complexity, although it would not remove the need for the contractual answers.

Ofelia West (VP Marketing): Right. And the account is large, but the buying group was not one unified entity. Regional teams had different appetites for risk and different launch dates.

Ofelia West (VP Marketing): Yes. Mark the opportunity lost, but do not characterize the security review as proof that the product failed security. We simply could not close the gaps in the time available.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That is clear. If we reconnect, I’ll start with the controlled response pack and a legal working session before proposing a new rollout date.

Ofelia West (VP Marketing): That would be the right order. For now, we are keeping the current tools in place and reassessing after the budget cycle.
