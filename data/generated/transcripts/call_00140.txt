Victor West (Privacy): CRM, website forms, event registration, and the customer portal. There are also distributor records, which are messy. Marketing wants to bring them into campaigns, but the legal basis and relationship are not always the same as for direct customers.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): We can model those as synchronized attributes and use them in audience rules. Which system should be authoritative for consent?

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Then one of the first workstreams should be a source-of-truth decision, with conflict handling defined before data is activated.

Victor West (Privacy): Yes. And the default behavior must be conservative. If the systems disagree, the platform should not select the more permissive value because it makes the campaign larger.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): We can design the synchronization so an unknown or conflicting status is excluded until reconciled. We would document that as a launch gate.

Victor West (Privacy): Good. What do you need from us to configure it?

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Field definitions, sample payloads, consent values, retention categories, and an example of a contact moving from prospect to customer. We also need to know whether deletion and access requests begin in the privacy service or in the CRM.

Victor West (Privacy): Both, depending on the request and the retention obligation. A person may ask us to stop marketing while we still need an order record. We cannot simply erase every linked object.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Then we need separate marketing suppression from broader data deletion. We should test that a suppressed person cannot re-enter through an overnight CRM sync.

Victor West (Privacy): That is one of my acceptance tests. Another is whether an event attendee who never consented can receive operational follow-up without being added to promotional nurture.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): We can separate transactional or event logistics from promotional journeys using purpose and consent fields. The content and legal wording still need your review.

Victor West (Privacy): Correct. The platform can enforce routing, but it cannot decide whether a particular message is truly operational.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Let us discuss retention. Do you have fixed periods by category?

Victor West (Privacy): We have a policy, but the periods differ by jurisdiction, relationship, and record type. Marketing would prefer one global setting because it is easier to manage.

Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): A single period may be simpler, but it could be too short for some business records and too long for some prospects. We should identify what the platform can automate and what remains a periodic review.

Victor West (Privacy): Exactly. I do not want a retention control that looks precise but is not aligned to the underlying policy. We need an exception report for records that cannot be automatically classified.
