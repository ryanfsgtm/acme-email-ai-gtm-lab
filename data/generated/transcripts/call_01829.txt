Lafayette Mayert (Revenue Operations): Building and changing journeys. A simple nurture can take three days because someone has to request a list, another person checks exclusions, and then we wait for a test send. We need to change a branch quickly when admissions rules change.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): So workflow speed and testing are the operator outcome, while attribution is the executive outcome.

Lafayette Mayert (Revenue Operations): Exactly. Please do not sell us a beautiful attribution dashboard and call the operational problem solved.

Lafayette Mayert (Revenue Operations): Can I compare two subject lines without creating two separate campaigns and manually stitching the results?

Lafayette Mayert (Revenue Operations): Missing identifiers are a real issue. We have prospective students appearing in the marketing system before they have an application ID.

Lafayette Mayert (Revenue Operations): That sounds right, although I need data governance to approve the retention period.
