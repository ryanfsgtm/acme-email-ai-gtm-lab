Clifford Heller (IT): CRM, our website forms, the service portal, and a data warehouse. There is also a partner channel that sends leads through an SFTP drop. It is not elegant, but it is stable.

Clifford Heller (IT): Marketing owns routine emails without filing tickets. IT has visibility into failures. Regional teams can see why a lead was routed. And nobody loses consent history during migration.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): On consent history, would you migrate all historical records or only active contacts?

Clifford Heller (IT): We need the history retained for audit, even if it is not used for campaigns. That is one place vendors oversimplify. “Archive the old data” is not an answer in a regulated environment.

Clifford Heller (IT): Reporting. The same lead can appear under multiple regional programs, and the attribution rules are difficult to explain. Sales does not trust the dashboard, so they export data and create their own.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That creates a governance problem as much as a reporting problem. Would you prefer a single global model or regional flexibility?

Clifford Heller (IT): A common core with controlled regional extensions. If every region can invent fields, we will be back here in two years.
