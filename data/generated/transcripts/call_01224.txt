Reginald Tillman (VP Marketing): Probably, provided it does not become a giant central process. Our teams need room to move quickly when a client asks for something specific.

Reginald Tillman (VP Marketing): A contact receiving multiple event invitations in the same week is annoying. More serious is when a client submits a request and continues receiving prospecting content. That makes us look disconnected.

Luis Romero (Customer Success Manager): Do you currently have a reliable event that identifies the request?

Reginald Tillman (VP Marketing): Not consistently. Some requests come through the website, some through a relationship manager, and some through a service mailbox. The mailbox is the weak point.

Reginald Tillman (VP Marketing): Agreed. Another possible expansion is analytics. The executive report tells me sends, clicks, and a broad pipeline number. It does not tell me whether engagement is improving account coverage.

Reginald Tillman (VP Marketing): Yes. We have bought reporting before and ended up with prettier versions of the same confusion.
