Marcus Chen (Account Executive): What happens after a referral comes in?

Trisha Haag-McKenzie (Revenue Operations): Front desk logs it in the practice system. Revenue operations exports a list every week, then I clean it and upload it to our email tool. Marcus Chen (Account Executive): That sounds like a lot of manual handoffs. What would a better process look like?

Trisha Haag-McKenzie (Revenue Operations): A new referral should receive the appropriate information, then stop if a staff member has already made contact. Existing patients should not get acquisition messaging. And I need to know which referral partners produce consultations, not just email opens.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): Which systems would need to connect?

Trisha Haag-McKenzie (Revenue Operations): Our practice management system, an old CRM, and the current email platform. The old CRM is technically alive but nobody trusts it. We also use spreadsheets because spreadsheets don't complain.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): Does Braze come up in the evaluation?

Trisha Haag-McKenzie (Revenue Operations): Yes. One adviser recommended it. It is powerful, but I am concerned about implementation. We cannot hire a lifecycle team to maintain it.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): What does your current tool do well?

Trisha Haag-McKenzie (Revenue Operations): It sends newsletters. That is about it. Basic automation exists, but the person who set it up left, and the workflows are not documented.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): How often would your team build or change journeys?

Trisha Haag-McKenzie (Revenue Operations): Probably weekly at first. Referral nurture, appointment reminders, reactivation, maybe a post-visit education series. The key is that I can change timing and branching without asking a developer.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): And what would leadership want to see?

Trisha Haag-McKenzie (Revenue Operations): A simple answer to whether marketing creates qualified consultations. Not vanity metrics. If a referral partner sends ten people and none book, I need that visible.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): We can map referral source through to consultation and report on conversion. We would want to confirm the source data is captured consistently, though.

Trisha Haag-McKenzie (Revenue Operations): Exactly. Automation cannot fix a field everyone types differently.

Trisha Haag-McKenzie (Revenue Operations): The weekly export. It takes me half a day, sometimes more. Then I worry that I have removed somebody from a campaign who should have stayed, or left somebody in who opted out.

Trisha Haag-McKenzie (Revenue Operations): Evidence that we reduce missed follow-up and can explain the source of consultations. He will ask about cost, of course, but also whether the system creates risk.
