Marcus Chen (Account Executive): That is a common pattern. How much of the technical work would sit with you?

Gwen Ebert (IT): Integration, permissions, security review, and probably the first import. Marketing would own the content. Sales operations is one person, part time, so I expect the practical burden lands here.

Gwen Ebert (Account Executive): We need a clear answer on processing locations, retention, deletion, and who can export contact data. We market in several European countries. Consent needs to travel with the record; an unsubscribe buried in an email is not enough for our internal review.

Gwen Ebert (Account Executive): Yes, if the fields are consistent. We could look at first touch within one business day, but not promise that every enquiry becomes a sales opportunity. Manufacturing cycles are long and uneven.
