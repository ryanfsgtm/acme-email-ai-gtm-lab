Nina Okafor (Solutions Consultant): Would you prefer automatic deletion, review queues, or both?

Jacinto Ernser (Privacy): Both. Automatic handling for clear cases, review for exceptions. The worst outcome is a silent deletion that removes an active relationship, but indefinite retention is also unacceptable.
