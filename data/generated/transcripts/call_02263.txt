Contacts can retain source and consent information, while campaign activity and product signals are available for segmentation. The point is to give the team one working view without requiring every source to be perfect on day one.

John O'Hara (VP Marketing): What does 'available' mean? Synced every few minutes, once a day, or manually uploaded?

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): In this workflow, we use a product event as a branch: active evaluators receive an educational sequence, while inactive accounts receive a different touch. The branch can also stop when an opportunity is created.

John O'Hara (VP Marketing): Stop automatically? What if the opportunity is created too early and sales has not spoken to the account yet?

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): Then the rule should not be simply 'opportunity exists.' You might use stage, owner, or a qualification flag. Automation follows the definition you choose; it cannot know whether the record was created as a placeholder.

John O'Hara (VP Marketing): That happens more often than I would like. Sales creates an opportunity to reserve a name, and marketing assumes there is a live buying process.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): We can account for that with a qualification threshold. For example, suppress broad nurture only after the opportunity reaches an agreed stage. Until then, marketing can coordinate rather than disappear.

John O'Hara (VP Marketing): That is closer to what we need. Show me the campaign builder.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): Here is a three-step program. First, an email with a technical guide. Second, a wait period. Third, a branch based on guide engagement and product activity. Each step has an audit trail, and the user can preview the audience before activating it.

John O'Hara (VP Marketing): Can the preview tell me why a person is included? I do not want to click through five screens to discover that they matched because a blank field was treated as 'all.'

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): Yes, the preview can expose the conditions and the matching values. We should also configure tests with known records, including records that should be excluded. That is the safer way to validate a new segment.

John O'Hara (VP Marketing): Add a test for a customer who is already subscribed. Our product team wants customer education, but those people should not be counted as new demand.

John O'Hara (VP Marketing): Reporting is where I am most skeptical. Vendors show a beautiful funnel, then every touch becomes influence. We lost a deal last year after six months of work, and the report still called the campaign successful because the contact attended a webinar.

Marcus Chen (Account Executive): Attribution should not erase outcome. We can display engagement, sourced pipeline, influenced pipeline, and closed revenue separately. If an opportunity is lost, it remains lost even if there was meaningful marketing activity.
