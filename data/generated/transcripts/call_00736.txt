Priya Shah (Account Executive): That makes sense. I remember your concern around migration effort. Has that concern reduced, or is it still the central objection?

Celine Huels (VP Marketing): It is still there. We have roughly thirty marketing and lifecycle users, plus agencies. A migration is not just moving campaigns. It is permissions, consent history, reporting definitions, and the integrations with our CRM and data warehouse. If one region has a different interpretation, the numbers stop being comparable.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): Would a limited regional rollout change the calculation?

Celine Huels (VP Marketing): Maybe. But our leadership prefers global standards. A small pilot can create a second operating model, which is exactly what they are trying to eliminate.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): What would need to happen for the project to become active again?

Celine Huels (VP Marketing): First, the data program needs a named technical lead. Second, security needs to approve the architecture before marketing spends time on workflows. Third, finance wants a clearer view of payback by region. We talked about all three, but we did not have them in that order.

Priya Shah (Account Executive): That ordering is helpful. Is there a target planning window?

Celine Huels (VP Marketing): We are doing another annual planning refresh in March. I would not call that a buying date. It is the next time we can put the question on the table with the right people present.
