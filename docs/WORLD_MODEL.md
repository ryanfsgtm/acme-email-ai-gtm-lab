# ACME Email World Model

ACME Email is a mature B2B marketing automation platform positioned between lightweight email tools and enterprise marketing clouds. It serves SMB, mid-market, and enterprise customers across an 18-month generated history.

## Product surface

- Campaign creation and delivery
- Lead capture, scoring, and segmentation
- Automated nurture journeys
- CRM synchronization
- Attribution and campaign reporting
- Forms and landing pages
- Consent management
- Dedicated IPs and deliverability tooling
- Enterprise roles, security, and governance

## Model layers

1. Canonical business truth
2. Account and buyer characteristics
3. Opportunity and lifecycle events
4. Call scenarios and participant goals
5. Transcript dialogue and transcription noise
6. Observable CRM outcomes

World generation must flow downward through these layers. It must not start by independently generating transcripts that mention random topics.

## Public analytical challenges

The corpus will contain common themes, rare commercially important signals, misleading correlations, seller-introduced topics, temporal shifts, persona differences, and findings that require joining multiple calls to CRM outcomes. Exact hidden truths and their prevalence remain in permanently private instructor materials.
